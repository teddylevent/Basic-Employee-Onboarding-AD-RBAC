NMG-0047 - Identity Configuration Resolution

Scenario: Fictional Northstar Medical Group lab
Affected user: Jane Cooper (jcooper@NMG.com)
Expected role: HR Payroll Specialist
Date resolved in original notes: September 17, 2026
Resolved by: IT New Hire (lab role)
Directory configuration rechecked: September 18, 2026

ISSUE REPORTED
Jane could sign in but received Access Denied when attempting to use HR shared
resources. Her desktop policies, mapped drives, and restrictions differed from
her HR teammates. The simulated ticket recorded that the password had been
checked, two restarts had not helped, and other HR users were unaffected.

INVESTIGATION AND ROOT CAUSE
Jane's account was located in the Operations OU. Her displayed memberships
included Domain Users and Operations-Users, with HR-Users missing. Those
departmental assignments did not match her HR Payroll Specialist role.

Two configuration issues required correction:
1. Incorrect organizational placement: Operations instead of HR.
2. Incorrect departmental membership: Operations-Users instead of HR-Users.

An incorrect group membership can explain an access failure when the target
resource grants permissions to the expected group. Incorrect OU placement can
affect applicable user policy when relevant GPOs are linked and in scope.
These are separate mechanisms; group membership does not automatically select
a departmental GPO, and an OU move alone does not grant resource access.

FIX APPLIED
- Moved Jane's account from the Operations OU to the HR OU.
- Removed Operations-Users membership.
- Added HR-Users membership while retaining Domain Users.
- Updated department to HR and job title to HR Payroll Specialist.
- Set her manager to Sandra Torres and company to NMG.

VERIFICATION
The retained local evidence shows Jane in the HR OU and her Member Of tab
showing Domain Users and HR-Users, with Operations-Users removed.

Read-only checks on September 18 independently confirmed:
- Distinguished name: CN=Jane Cooper,OU=HR,DC=NMG,DC=com
- Department: HR
- Title: HR Payroll Specialist
- Departmental MemberOf value: CN=HR-Users,OU=HR,DC=NMG,DC=com
- Manager: CN=Sandra Torres,OU=HR,DC=NMG,DC=com

The original Day 4 notes record that Jane restarted, signed in again, and
confirmed that access and her desktop experience were corrected. This remains
a reported outcome within the simulated ticket. End-to-end resource access,
share permissions, mapped drives, and resultant workstation policy were not
independently tested for this portfolio.

The Day 5 GPO inventory showed only Default Domain Policy and Default Domain
Controllers Policy. Accordingly, this report establishes the corrected
identity configuration and does not claim a verified departmental GPO rollout.

PREVENTION
Use a documented onboarding review that checks the employee's department,
title, manager, OU, and approved group memberships together. After a transfer
or correction, remove obsolete memberships and test the required resources
from the user's session before treating access restoration as fully verified.
